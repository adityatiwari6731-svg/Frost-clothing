Maison / Heritage & Craft

The Hands & Savoir-Faire Behind Frost

Preserving centuries of Indian handloom nobility. Where antique silver threads and natural silks intertwine in the sacred ateliers of Varanasi, Kanchipuram, and Kashmir.

Chapter I · The Sacred Looms of Kashi

Where Silver Weaves With Silence

In the narrow stone lanes flanking the Ganges, generations of master weavers wake before dawn. The rhythmic clack of the wooden pit loom has echoed here for six hundred years.

At Frost, we refuse industrial high-speed jacquards. Every single saree is woven using the laborious Kadwa technique — where each botanical leaf and royal peacock motif is individually hand-interlocked with silver and gold zari without a single floating thread left on the reverse.

Chapter II · The Alchemical Frost Luster

Crafting the Celestial Sheen

Our signature icy-platinum tissue silk requires an extraordinarily rare yarn twisting process. Ultra-fine mulberry silk filaments are enveloped in microscopic electroplated silver ribbons before being introduced to the warp.

The result is a fabric that does not merely reflect light — it radiates an ethereal moonlit frost that moves like liquid mercury across the silhouette.

Chapter III · The Living Sanctuary

Elevating Daily Life to an Art Form

We believe the fabrics closest to your skin should possess the same aristocratic pedigree as royal court couture. Our bedsheets use solely Giza long-staple Egyptian cotton harvested by hand along the Nile Delta.

Spun into 800-thread-count single-ply sateen, every sheet undergoes double silver satin stitching by our master tailors to guarantee five-star boutique sanctuary sleep for decades.

The 45-Day Metamorphosis

From Cocoon to Finished Drape

True luxury cannot be rushed. Explore the meticulous steps behind every Frost creation.

Day 01 – Day 07

Ethical Sericulture & Silk Reeling

Natural mulberry cocoons are carefully sorted for long continuous filaments, then spun into Grade 6A pure katan silk yarn.

Day 08 – Day 16

Pure Silver Zari Wire Flattening

Fine silver ingots are drawn through diamond dies into micro-threads, flattened, and gilded with 24-karat gold for tested zari authenticity.

Day 17 – Day 25

Naqsha Master Graph & Loom Warping

The master naqshaband translates poetic heritage patterns onto graph paper and hand-threads thousands of warp cords into the pit loom.

Day 26 – Day 42

The Kadwa Weaving Devotion

Two master weavers sit side by side, advancing just 2 to 3 inches per day, individually locking each silver motif by hand.

Day 43 – Day 45

Silk Mark Inspection & Presentation

Tested by the Central Silk Board of India, issued with a unique serial hologram, hand-ironed, and encased in an embossed gold-foil trunk.
