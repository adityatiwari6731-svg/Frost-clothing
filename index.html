The Heritage Lineage

The Weavers of Frost Atelier

Born from a reverence for the golden age of Indian weaving, FROST bridges ancient pit-loom artistry with contemporary architectural aesthetics. Every saree is woven thread-by-thread by fourth-generation master craftspeople across Varanasi and Kanchipuram.

Our home collection mirrors this uncompromising standard: 100% natural long-staple Egyptian cotton woven to an authentic 800-thread count, paired with velvet cushions hand-embroidered by royal zardozi artisans.

Pure Tested Zari

Certified silver and electroplated gold metallic threads that never tarnish over generations.

Sustainable Living

Eco-friendly organic dyes, zero microplastics, and direct fair-trade artisan profit sharing.
