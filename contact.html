Frequently Answered Enquiries

Details regarding delivery, authenticity, and bespoke customization across India.

How does White-Glove Shipping across India work? +
All orders above ₹20,000 enjoy complimentary insured express courier across all 28 states and Union Territories via BlueDart Apex and Delhivery Express. Every Frost piece arrives in an embossed, archival keepsake presentation trunk with hand-tied satin ribbons and a tamper-evident gold seal.
Are blouse stitching and fall & pico edge-finishing included? +
Yes, complimentary fall & pico finishing is provided on every couture saree prior to dispatch. Each saree also comes with a matching unstitched satin silk blouse piece. If you desire our master tailors to custom-stitch the blouse according to your exact measurements, select the Bespoke Tailoring option at checkout.
How should I store and care for pure silk and zari sarees? +
Always wrap your pure katan and Kanjivaram silks in breathable unbleached muslin cotton cloth bags. Never store them in plastic. Dry clean only with a reputable specialist. Avoid spraying perfumes, alcohol-based cosmetics, or water directly onto electroplated silver zari to preserve its heirloom luster.
Can I order bespoke bedsheet sizes or custom banquet tablecloth dimensions? +
Absolutely. Our Home Sanctuary atelier crafts made-to-measure Egyptian cotton bedsheet sets for non-standard mattresses, mega-king beds, luxury yachts, and private residences. Please select "Residence Living" on our booking form or message our concierge directly.
What is your return and authentication guarantee? +
Every creation is backed by a 100% pure silk authenticity guarantee and certified by the Central Silk Board of India with a scannable QR hologram. Unaltered, unstitched heirlooms with security tags intact may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of receipt.
